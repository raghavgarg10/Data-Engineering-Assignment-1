# Business Insights

## Key Findings

* Technology category generated the highest sales revenue among all categories.
* Top customers contributed a significant share of overall sales.
* Sales performance varied across regions, with the West region generating the highest sales.
* Monthly sales trends showed fluctuations throughout the year.
* Data quality checks found no missing values in the Sales column.
* Duplicate Order IDs were present, which is expected because a single order can contain    multiple products.

## Conclusion

The analysis provided valuable insights into sales performance, customer behavior, and regional trends. These findings can help businesses improve customer targeting, optimize product strategies, and support data-driven decision-making.
